# And Finally: Today's TV Guide

This will be my last post before the festive season.  So until 2010, I heartily recommend that you catch the [latest episode of Horizon](http://www.bbc.co.uk/iplayer/episode/b00pdjmk/Horizon_20092010_How_Many_People_Can_Live_on_Planet_Earth/).  Now, I'm not normally a fan of the series - they have a habit of turning science into relentless melodrama (see their [episode about Gamma Ray Bursts](http://www.bbc.co.uk/science/horizon/2001/deathstartrans.shtml) to see what I mean - John Shrapnel's narration only heightens the sense of dizzying hysteria they attempted to evoke).  However, their latest effort rents its credibility from the highest source - the legend that is David Attenborough.  David talks about the problems of human population.  We're fast approaching 7 billion on the planet - how many more can we handle?  The program is a balanced, yet still jaw-dropping account of how far we are living outside our means.
Thanks for reading, Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year!
