# Beaten to the Punch by Al

Sometimes you want to tear your hair out about climate change denialism, and write a massive rant...then you realise that someone has done something much better, and [written a well constructed article](http://www.nytimes.com/2010/02/28/opinion/28gore.html?pagewanted=3&sq=al%20gore&st=cse&scp=2) in the New York Times that calmly takes down the arguments of the deniers, and underlines the importance of action, and rational thought over anecdotalism and apoplexy.  Plus, he happens to be Al Gore, and much more influential and eloquent than I.  Take it away, Al.
