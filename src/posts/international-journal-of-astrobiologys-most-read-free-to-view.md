# International Journal of Astrobiology's Most Read Free to View

I've just received an email stating that the [International Journal of Astrobiology](http://journals.cambridge.org/action/displayJournal?jid=IJA) is offering free access to its most read papers of 2009. So have a look and see - there's some really interesting research going on there.  And this post [is not in the least bit self-serving](http://journals.cambridge.org/action/displayAbstract?fromPage=online&aid=5981608&fulltextType=RA&fileId=S1473550408004321) either!
