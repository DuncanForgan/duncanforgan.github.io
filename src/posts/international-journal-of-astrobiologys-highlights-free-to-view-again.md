# International Journal of Astrobiology's Highlights Free to View (Again)

Guess who's there [again](http://wellbredinsolence.wordpress.com/2010/01/15/international-journal-of-astrobiologys-most-read-free-to-view/ "International Journal of Astrobiology’s Most Read Free to View")? Check out the International Journal of Astrobiology's [Highlights of 2010](http://bit.ly/ftO98O) for free.  While my entry is obviously worth your attention (and you can find a blog post about it [here](http://wellbredinsolence.wordpress.com/2010/05/13/is-entropy-the-secret-to-intelligence/ "Is Entropy the secret to Intelligence?")), there are plenty of others to check out too - including an early discussion of arsenic as a potential biochemical substitute (well before the NASA press release madness of last December).
